01 / The question

Beyond existing
boundaries.

What becomes possible when intelligence can do more than repeat what is already known?

Fluid Intelligence exists to explore and advance the possibilities of artificial intelligence. We are interested in systems capable of deeper reasoning, independent investigation, complex problem-solving and original creation.

Our ambition is to contribute to a future in which artificial intelligence expands what humanity can discover, understand and accomplish.

AI is the means. Human progress is the objective.
A longer horizon

A future where new forms of intelligence help us reach new scientific discoveries, technological breakthroughs and artistic expression.

02 / What we believe

An open horizon.

A research and development company focused on one enduring question: how far can intelligence take human understanding?

I — PURPOSE

Capability in service of possibility.

We pursue advances in AI not as an end in themselves, but for the discoveries, solutions and creative possibilities they may make available to humanity.

II — PHILOSOPHY

Beyond what is already known.

Intelligence should not be limited to processing the familiar. Its greatest potential lies in discovering what remains unknown and creating what does not yet exist.

III — AMBITION

Progress that expands us.

Our long-term ambition is to help build increasingly capable intelligent systems that extend human scientific, technological and artistic endeavor.

03 / Directions of inquiry

Research &
development.

These are directions we intend to investigate and advance. They describe an ambition, not completed research or established results.

01

Advanced Artificial Intelligence

Developing more capable AI systems with improved reasoning, learning, problem-solving and generalisation abilities.

02

Autonomous Scientific Discovery

Exploring systems that investigate scientific questions, formulate hypotheses, evaluate evidence and identify unknown relationships.

03

Mathematical Discovery

Advancing AI capabilities in complex mathematical reasoning, original proofs and the exploration of new mathematical ideas and frameworks.

04

Fundamental Scientific Research

Exploring how AI can contribute to deeper understanding in physics, biology, chemistry and other scientific disciplines.

05

Cross-Disciplinary Discovery

Investigating how intelligent systems can connect knowledge across fields and surface insights obscured by disciplinary boundaries.

06

Autonomous Research Intelligence

Exploring iterative research systems that plan investigations, use computational tools and improve their research processes.

07

AI and Artistic Advancement

Exploring how increasingly capable intelligent systems can expand creative processes, artistic expression and human imagination.
